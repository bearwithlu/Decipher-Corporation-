Welcome to Decipher Corp., Agent.

Decipher Corp. is an interactive, fictional corporate system built around the idea of a company that may be hiding more than it reveals. Through employee onboarding, internal communications, encrypted transmissions, archived records, and corporate services, users can explore the system and uncover what lies beneath its official surface.

What begins as an ordinary employee interface may lead to something much less ordinary.

Your access has been granted. What you do with it is up to you.

⸻

About the Project

Decipher Corp. is an experimental interactive experience developed as an Apple Shortcuts project. It combines local data storage, dynamic content, fictional corporate infrastructure, and an evolving narrative into a single system.

The project is designed to grow over time. New content, records, transmissions, and discoveries can be introduced through updates, allowing the experience to expand without requiring every piece of content to be built directly into the main shortcut.

Features

* Employee Registration
    Register a username and receive a unique Decipher Corp. employee ID (DC-ID).
* Personalized Employee Profile
    Maintain individual employee settings and custom symbols for confirmations, negative responses, and notifications.
* Employee ID Card
    Access your personalized corporate identification.
* Corporate Intranet
    Explore the internal services and information available to registered employees.
* Decipher Mail
    Access the company’s fictional internal mail system.
* Debriefing & Internal News
    Discover company updates, reports, and potentially relevant information.
* Receive Transmission
    Receive incoming transmissions from the system.
* Decrypt It
    Investigate encoded messages and uncover their contents.
* Encrypt It
    Encode messages within the system.
* Archive
    Keep track of archived information and records.
* Decider Invest
    Explore the company’s fictional investment service.
* Persistent Local Data
    Employee information and system state are stored locally through Apple Shortcuts, keeping user-side data separate from remotely hosted content libraries.
* Expandable Content System
    The project is designed to support future content additions and updates through external libraries and configuration files.

Project Philosophy

Decipher Corp. is built around exploration, discovery, and the gradual expansion of an interconnected fictional world.

Not every piece of information needs to be presented directly. Some details may be introduced through internal communications, updates, or discoveries elsewhere in the system.

The goal is to create an experience that rewards curiosity and encourages users to look beyond the surface.

Technology

The project currently uses:

* Apple Shortcuts for the interactive system and its workflows.
* JSON for structured data, configuration, and content libraries.
* GitHub for version control and remote content hosting.
* Local storage for user-specific data and persistent system state.

The architecture is intended to separate the main shortcut, user data, and remotely maintained content wherever practical.

Disclaimer

Decipher Corp. is a personal hobby project created in my free time, mostly out of curiosity and whenever I’m bored enough to start experimenting with something new.

The idea is simple: to see how far Apple Shortcuts can be pushed when you get creative with it.

What started as an experiment has gradually grown into a fictional corporate system with its own infrastructure, employee records, communications, and secrets. It’s a fun way to explore the possibilities and limitations of Apple Shortcuts, experiment with automation, and see what can be built using tools that were never necessarily intended for something like this.

This project is developed for fun, learning, and experimentation. It is not a commercial product, professional software solution, or official Apple project. Features, functionality, and content may change as I continue experimenting and finding new ways to push the limits of Shortcuts.

In other words, this is what happens when boredom meets automation.

Installation & Updates

Installation and distribution are handled through Apple Shortcuts.

Follow the installation instructions provided alongside the project. Updates and additional content may be distributed through the project’s GitHub repository.

Some features may require an internet connection to retrieve remotely hosted content.

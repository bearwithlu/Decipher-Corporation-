Decipher Corp. 🏢

Decipher Corp. is an interactive, narrative-driven game built entirely in Apple Shortcuts. Step into the role of a Decipher Corp. employee and explore an internal corporate network filled with encrypted codes, confidential documents, internal communications, and hidden secrets.

Features

* 🪪 Employee ID System – Generate a unique employee ID and view your personnel information.
* 🖥️ Corporate Intranet – Explore internal company pages, news, mail, and contract details.
* 🔐 Code Generation & Decoding – Receive randomly generated codes and solve them through the decoding system.
* 💳 Account Balance – Track your in-game credits through the employee terminal.
* 📂 Internal Records & Lore – Discover the company’s world through documents, reports, and hidden information.
* ⚙️ Personalization & Settings – Customize parts of your employee interface and manage your local game data.
* 🌐 HTML-Based Interface – Experience a stylized corporate terminal with a dark, purple-accented design.

Concept

What begins as an ordinary employee terminal gradually opens the door to a deeper mystery. Explore, decode, investigate, and pay attention to the details. Not everything in the system may be as straightforward as it appears.

Built with Apple Shortcuts. No dedicated app required.
